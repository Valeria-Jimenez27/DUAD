The Animal Kingdom

An introduction to three great species of the natural world

Animal Image Description Data
Bear
Ursus arctos		 The bear is one of the largest and most powerful mammals on the planet. It inhabits forests, mountains, and tundras of the Northern Hemisphere, adapting remarkably well to both cold and temperate climates. It is known for its intelligence, extraordinary sense of smell, and winter hibernation cycle.
  • Weight: 100 – 680 kg
  • Length: 1.5 – 2.8 m
  • Diet: Fruits, fish, insects, honey, and carrion
  • Life expectancy: 20 – 30 years
Lion
Panthera leo		 Known as the "king of the jungle", the lion is the only big cat that lives in social groups called prides. It is native to the African savanna, where cubs begin learning to hunt from an early age. Its roar can be heard from more than 8 kilometers away.
  • Weight: 120 – 250 kg
  • Length: 1.7 – 2.5 m
  • Diet: Zebras, wildebeest, antelopes, and buffaloes
  • Life expectancy: 10 – 16 years
Shark
Carcharodon carcharias		 The shark is one of the oldest and most efficient predators in the ocean, with over 400 million years of evolution. It possesses a unique sensory system capable of detecting electric fields, making it an extraordinarily precise hunter. It plays a vital ecological role in maintaining the balance of marine ecosystems.
  • Weight: 700 – 2,200 kg
  • Length: 4 – 6 m
  • Diet: Fish, seals, dolphins, and sea turtles
  • Life expectancy: 30 – 70 years
