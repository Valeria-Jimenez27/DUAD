Blogging is far from dead. As the last 12 months of Twitter—sorry, X—drama have shown, allowing a social media platform to control your audience has risks. If you want a platform where you can share your thoughts properly and keep control of things, it's impossible to beat a blog. Plus, you can always share your blog posts on social media.

Similarly, if you run a business and want to provide resources and recommendations to customers, the best way to do it is often to add a blog to your website. Best of all, your blog content gets indexed by Google—unlike almost all social media posts—so you can drive potential customers to your business through content marketing (and without having to pay for ads). Look at the blog you're reading right now. Zapier blog posts get millions of views per month and are one of the most valuable ways of getting new customers at Zapier. That kind of organic reach is hard to replicate on any social platform, where algorithms control who sees your content and when. A blog puts you in charge of your own distribution, your own SEO, and ultimately your own growth—without depending on a feed that changes overnight.
