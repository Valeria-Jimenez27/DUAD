Animal Kingdom

Discover the wild

Lion

Panthera leo

Known as the "King of the Jungle," the lion is a social big cat that lives in groups called prides across sub-Saharan Africa.

  • Habitat Savanna & Grasslands
  • Weight 120–250 kg
  • Status Vulnerable

Giraffe

Giraffa camelopardalis

The tallest living terrestrial animal on Earth, the giraffe uses its long neck to reach leaves high in the treetops of Africa.

  • Habitat Savanna & Woodland
  • Weight 750–1,270 kg
  • Status Vulnerable

Rhinoceros

Ceratotherium simum

One of the largest land animals, the rhinoceros is known for its distinctive horn and thick protective skin across African plains.

  • Habitat Grasslands & Shrublands
  • Weight 1,400 –3,600 kg
  • Status Near Threatened
