What is social media?

Social media refers to the apps and websites that allow people to interact with other users, businesses, communities, and content. It has revolutionized how people socialize, shop, and consume news across platforms like social networks, media-sharing apps, and discussion forums. With nearly 60 percent of the world's population using social media, its influence on daily life and global business continues to grow at an unprecedented pace.

Conclusion

Social media has fundamentally transformed the way people communicate, consume information, and interact with brands. With billions of active users worldwide, platforms like Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram have become essential tools for both personal expression and business growth. Their influence continues to shape culture, politics, and the global economy.
